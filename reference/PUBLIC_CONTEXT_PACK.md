# Public context pack

## Purpose

This compact context is safe for the development test. It contains no personal data, school-specific operational information or unpublished document text.

## European Schools context

The European Schools provide multilingual and multicultural education for pupils from diverse backgrounds. Syllabi are harmonised at system level, while teaching and local implementation must remain responsive to age, language section, inclusion needs and school context.

The application should therefore:

- preserve the subject and cycle-specific purpose of a syllabus;
- distinguish official curriculum requirements from optional implementation suggestions;
- support competence-based learning and assessment;
- recognise multilingual, multicultural and inclusive participation as design conditions;
- allow school and teacher contextualisation without presenting optional ideas as system rules;
- favour interdisciplinary and whole-school connections where they add genuine learning value;
- respect teacher professional judgement.

## GreenComp use

GreenComp is the European sustainability competence framework. It contains four areas and 12 competences. It is non-prescriptive: competences can be developed in different subjects, ages and contexts, and every unit does not need to cover all 12.

The tool should look for coherent evidence across:

- rationale and values;
- learning objectives/outcomes;
- content and inquiry questions;
- learning activities and pupil participation;
- action and stakeholder engagement;
- assessment evidence and reflection.

## Whole-school connection

A whole-school approach connects teaching and learning with participation, school culture, operations, leadership, staff learning and community relationships. A syllabus review should make these connections only when educationally relevant and age-appropriate. It must not turn every subject into an operational sustainability plan.

## Interpretation boundaries

- Do not invent a European Schools obligation.
- Do not treat recycling vocabulary as embedded GreenComp learning by itself.
- Do not treat group work as collective action when decisions and actions are predetermined.
- Do not treat a poster as political agency unless pupils engage an authentic decision route or stakeholder.
- Do not assess pupils according to whether a school-level environmental outcome was achieved outside their control.
